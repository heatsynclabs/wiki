## Concept

After a [Nov 2010](http://www.cell.com/current-biology/abstract/S0960-9822(10)01281-9) article in the journal Current Biology (no pun intended I'm sure) brought to our attention the technique of running a small amount of DC current across the surface of the head to induce positive (or negative) effects. It's our intention to build a device similar to the [one used in the study](http://www.neuroconn.de/en/solutions/dc-stimulator_en/), and attempt to reproduce the findings.
