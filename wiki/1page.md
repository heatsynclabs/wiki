<big>**From [YouTube™](http://www.youtube.com) to [Etsy™](http://www.etsy.com/) to [Arduino™](http://www.arduino.cc/), the Internet has created an entire generation of users who choose to break free from the traditional consumer model and instead aspire to create and share.**</big> Traditional institutions have failed to provide adequate resources to meet these new demands. While the cost of digital production has fallen to nearly zero, access to low cost professional quality tools has not followed suit. Heavy industrial and electrical equipment, as well as professional computer software packages, can be so costly as to be out of reach for the student, entrepreneur, or garage tinkerer. We at HeatSync Labs seek to make these resources available to our creative community through cooperative collaboration. In addition, we believe that it's not enough to merely provide access to these resources, but that it's just as important to foster a community of sharing and educating among members.\
