# On HeatSync Labs

I believe in advancing the following characteristics of HeatSync Labs, until I hear of better ones.

## Vision (Why)

Hacking is the art of discovering what is possible. At HeatSync Labs, we gather together to hack.

## Mission (What)

At HeatSync Labs, we create an environment that gives people joy in making.

## Principles (How)

### Integrity

HeatSync Labs does good on its own terms. We hope people and orgs will recognize that and support us. We do not pander to money. Our benefactors support us because we serve our mission, not theirs.

### Honesty

HeatSync Labs is open and honest about what we do and where we are going. We encourage our community to work with us to open new paths forward.

### Positivity

At HeatSync Labs we strive to create a positive environment and to communicate respectfully.
