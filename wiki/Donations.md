## Donations

Interested people/companies may choose to make financial contributions to Heatsync Labs that extend beyond typical membership/automatic donations. These interested parties will be able to make donations using the following mechanisms:

### Check

Checks can be made payable to Heatsync Labs, and given to any active member. Active members will then place the check into the drop safe sitting on the east wall of the lab (in the shelves). The drop safe will be emptied, and checks deposited twice a month.

### Cash

Cash can be given to any active member, who will then place the cash into an envelope (envelopes are available on the top of the safe), make a note on the envelope, and place the envelope into the drop safe located on the east wall of the lab.

### Paypal

Our paypal address is hslfinances@gmail.com (do not send emails to this address, it is not monitored actively) -- paypal donations may be sent to this address.

### BitCoin

This will deposit bitcoin into our coinbase account. If you would like this to be immediately converted to USD, please email the current treasurer and ask them to withdraw the funds.
