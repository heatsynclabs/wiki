Hackerspaces get discounts at certain vendors. The following is a list:

- [Adafruit](http://www.adafruit.com/) - Up to 40% off depending on quantity. \$250 Minimum
  - [link](http://www.adafruit.com/blog/2010/06/01/big-news-all-hacker-spaces-in-the-world-get-adafruit-reseller-pricing-starting-today/)
- [Makerbot](http://www.makerbot.com/) 5% off a makerbot
  - Have to contact somebody at Makerbot with your credentials
- [Sparkfun](http://www.sparkfun.com/) - We have gotten 20% off on large buys in the past.
- [Arduino Reseller](http://www.magyc-now.com/catalog/index.php?main_page=products_new) - We are an official Arduino distributor. We've found you must order in major quantities to make the shipping worth it though. We tend to use the Sparkfun or Adafruit discounts first.
