2nd and 4th Thursdays of each month at 7pm at the hackerspace. HYH is the members' night to get stuff done around the space. It is NOT a discussion time, but a time when we gather to GET SHIT DONE and make our space better. That includes voting on proposals, helping out at the lab, helping with community projects, and planning projects.

**Before the meeting:**

- Proposals for voting are posted on the email list board immediately following HYH for the next HYH period
- Proposals locked 1 week before HYH
- Proposals should be small in scope, operational, and actionable.
- Members have the ability to dismiss a proposal because they feel it's too broad and should be handled by the board
- Proposals should be discussed in depth on the forums prior to HYH

**At the meeting:**

- Any member can begin the meeting promptly at 7, with or without a quorum of 8 members.
- The first part is the vote; voting happens quickly, generally without discussion. If there's no quorum, the vote may be skipped or delayed til later in the meeting.
- Log the vote in the minutes section below.
- The second part is getting stuff done; break out to clean/fix/replace/action anything else that has to happen at the lab. For example, may have broken equipment per station that needs to be evaluated. Is our stock of cups/tp/cleaning products sufficient for 2 weeks?
- Review storage and process incoming donations and decide what to keep/store fix or sell, or recycle/throw away.
- Review the lost and found box.
