2nd and 4th Thursdays of each month at 7pm at the hackerspace. HYH is an all hands night to get stuff done around the space. It is NOT a discussion time, but a time when we gather to GET SHIT DONE and make our space better. That includes voting on proposals, helping out at the lab, helping with community projects, and planning projects.

- Proposals for voting are posted on the email list board immediately following HYH for the next HYH period
- Proposals locked 1 week before HYH
- Proposals should be small in scope, operational, and actionable. Should not pertain to overall mission of operation.
- Members have the ability to pick out a proposal at Lock period because they feel its not operations based
- Proposals should be discussed in depth on the forums prior to HYH
- Vote happens quickly generally without discussion at beginning of HYH
- Then break out to clean/fix/replace/action anything else that has to happen at the lab. For example, may have broken equipment per station that needs to be evaluated. Is our stock of cups/tp/cleaning products sufficient for 2 weeks?
- Review storage and process incoming donations and decide what to keep/store fix or sell, or recycle/throw away.
- Process lost and found box
