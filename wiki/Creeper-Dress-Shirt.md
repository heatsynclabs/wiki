Dress shirt, laser-cut, hand-sewn, with custom printed fabric.

Fabric based on Creeper texture straight from the Minecraft videogame, printed at [http://www.spoonflower.com/fabric/892733 SpoonFlower.com (click for texture)](http://www.spoonflower.com/fabric/892733_SpoonFlower.com_(click_for_texture))

Shirt design based on existing shirt I had, tore apart, scanned using a large copier, traced in Inkscape, laser cut the fabric, and uploaded the shirt design to [http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:13434 Thingiverse (click for DXF design files & instructions)](http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:13434_Thingiverse_(click_for_DXF_design_files_&_instructions)).

Sewed using normal sewing machine techniques (see Thingiverse for sewing instructions.)
