Dress shirt, laser-cut, hand-sewn, with custom printed fabric.

Fabric based on Creeper texture straight from the Minecraft videogame, printed at [SpoonFlower.com (click for texture)](http://www.spoonflower.com/fabric/892733)

Shirt design based on existing shirt I had, tore apart, scanned using a large copier, traced in Inkscape, laser cut the fabric, and uploaded the shirt design to [Thingiverse (click for design)](http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:13434).

Sewed using normal sewing machine techniques (see Thingiverse for sewing instructions.)
