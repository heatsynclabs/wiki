Members, guests, and even lab administration need regular restocks of some basic supplies to keep going.

# Reimbursement

Members can purchase and donate these basic supplies at any time, and we are grateful!

In some circumstances we can reimburse purchases, with some requirements:

- Seek approval from an appropriate board member first.
- E-mail a receipt to finances at heatsynclabs dot org.

Reimbursement is not guaranteed so these purchases are made at the member's own risk.

# Supplies

Here's some info on supplies we need regularly:

- Paper towels
  - Towel dispensers take industrial hardwound (not center-pull) towels, 7.75 to 8 inches wide. Both 600 and 800-foot rolls have worked in the past.
  - Because roll length and number of rolls per box vary, it's best to calculate cost per foot (not square foot, as all compatible towels will be about 8 inches wide).
  - Brands that have worked previously:
    - GEN (Amazon, \$0.0077 per foot)
    - First Street (Smart & Final, \$0.0083 per foot)
- Toilet paper
- Hand Soap
  - Dispensers have previously been stocked with Maker's Mark Foaming Antibacterial Hand Soap 1000ml "bag in box" refills.
  - This brand may be the only kind of refill compatible with the bathroom dispensers, due to the shape of the nozzle and the way it clips into the dispenser.
  - The only direct seller of Member's Mark brand is Sam's Club. If you see Member's Mark elsewhere, it's being sold by a third-party reseller, probably at a significant markup.
