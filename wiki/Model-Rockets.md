Chandler

Mesquite Grove Park at Riggs and Val Vista is the only park where you are allowed to shoot off model rockets. You must make a special request 10 days prior to the event and can pick up the permit at the Tumbleweed Recreation Area or at the Parks and Recreation office

Gilbert

Crossroads Park A,B,C & D engines allowed. Must have a 100-500ft clearance from ANYTHING

McQueen Park A & B Engines only 100-200ft clearance

Freestone Park A,B,C & D engines allowed. Must have a 100-500ft clearance from anything

At all places you must have a Ranger approve of your launch site

You can pick up an information sheet for model rockets at the Parks & Rec office at building 90. I guess it gives you all necessary information.
