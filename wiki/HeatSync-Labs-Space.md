This page is intended to document the peculiarities and maintenance of the suite in which HeatSync Labs physically exists, currently 108 W Main St in downtown Mesa.

# Maintenance

## Supply Closet

Located beside the stairs, next to the kitchen.

\- Stores our brooms, mops, and other cleaning equipment. - The drain pipe in this closet leads, of course, to the sewer. The trap in this drain is supposed to have water in the bottom. But sometimes it can dry out, allowing foul-smelling sewer gases to leak into the lab. If this happens, pour about 6 cups of water into the drain. That should re-fill the trap and stop the smell.
