## Acquisition

Lets pick out a list of 'essential' tools in each category. The [Fab Lab](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_lab) might be a good starting point for this. For those that are prohibitively expensive, lets choose the ACTUAL tool we ACTUALLY want to buy and post a kickstarter campaign for it.
