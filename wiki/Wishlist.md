## Acquisition

Some facts:

- Leasing looks to be impossibly for a business less than 2 years old. It may be possible to borrow against a single member's credit. Any takers?
- Corporate donations are few and far between until our 501c3 clears which could take 6 months.
- Current donation drives have been stagnant possibly as a result of their vagueness.
- Kickstarter and other microlending sites look a promising way to fundraise for a very specific goal.

Lets pick out a list of 'essential' tools in each category. The [Fab Lab](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fab_lab) might be a good starting point for this. For those that are prohibitively expensive, lets choose the ACTUAL tool we ACTUALLY want to buy and post a kickstarter campaign for it.

For cheaper tools [ Auctions](Local-Resources#auctions), [Craigslist](http://www.craigslist.com) and [Cheap Chinese Tools](Cheap-Chinese-Tools) are probably the way to go.
