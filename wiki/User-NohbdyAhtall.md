== GPD, GPDMCC, Game Programming & Development ==
A club, phyle, and/or template for other clubs. GPD and GPDMCC tend to refer to the club I founded in 2014.
=== GPD as a ClubDev Template ===
The goal of GPDMCC is to build up a template or "starter-pack" of sorts, for others to create their own clubs. Since GPDMCC will likely be outputting public domain knowledge, information, and tools for others to start their own clubs. It is also planned that there is a sort of internal network of sharing, where a threshold of aligning to each-other opens up more direct communication and sharing. (Threshold_Example): Assuming GPDT for the template, GPDMCC for my club, and GPDMPE for a Money, Profit, and Employment focus there may be reason(or not) for GPDMCC and GPDMPE to disconnect from the network. An example being that GPDMPE has been focusing on "making money" but without relying on intellectual property or paywalling members, but once they cross the threshold of making changes that embrace those concepts, GPDMCC and neighboring clubs disconnect GPDMPE from the "network" of shared comms, asset workflow access, donation fund streams, or anything of that nature. It needn't imply a negative disconnect, but is possible
