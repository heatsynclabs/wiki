## When

Saturday 03/09/2024 any time from 11am - 5pm

This is during I Love Mesa Day when there will be a lot going on downtown.

## Description

Come have fun making and playing games. We will be making games, puzzles, and toys with the laser cutter, 3d printer, soldering irons and other tools in the lab. This free event is for everyone. Anyone under 18 must have an adult with them.

## History

We did a [Fun and Games Design Challenge](https://guestlist.co/events/736986) on 02/19/2023. The intention was to create files for projects we could then turn around and use during Fun and Games Day. There were not many people signed up.
